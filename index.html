About Me

My Journey

I'm an entrepreneur, investor, and mentor with over 16 years of experience navigating the financial markets. My story began humbly: as a teenager, working my first cash-in-hand job as a gardener, depositing my wages into my IG Index trading account, and placing trades from my Blackberry phone. Even then, I was committed to building something greater.

By the time I was at university, I was managing six-figure positions in forex options — a rare experience that taught me how to think and act under pressure. I can confidently say I've seen it all: bull markets, bear markets, and everything in between.

Beyond trading, I scaled a successful construction business to 17 employees, sharpening my skills as both a businessman and a leader. Yet my greatest achievements came when I fully committed to crypto in 2017 — dedicating myself full-time, studying relentlessly, and mastering an emerging financial frontier. That decision made me a multimillionaire and gave me the freedom to design life on my own terms.

Mind Over Matter

Mind over matter is not a slogan to me; it's a way of life. I believe in taking action fast, failing fast, and learning even faster — a system that accelerates results and compresses time frames to success.

Today, I channel my energy into helping others achieve their breakthroughs — whether in wealth creation, mindset transformation, or building a lifestyle of freedom and fulfillment.
