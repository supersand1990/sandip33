My Journey

I'm an entrepreneur, investor, and mentor with over 16 years of experience navigating the financial markets. My story began humbly: as a teenager, working my first cash-in-hand job as a gardener, depositing my wages into my IG Index trading account, and placing trades from my Blackberry phone. Even then, I was committed to building something greater.

By the time I was at university, I was managing six-figure positions in forex options — a rare experience that taught me how to think and act under pressure. I can confidently say I've seen it all: bull markets, bear markets, and everything in between.
